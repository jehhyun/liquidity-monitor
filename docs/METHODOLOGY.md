# Liquidity Monitor — Conditional Logic Methodology\n\n_Last corrected: 2026-08-31. This file is the durable reference for how risk-off\ntriggers and asset headwinds are classified. Any future refresh (manual or\nscheduled) should follow this, not single-indicator thresholds._\n\n## Core principle\n\nReplace binary `INDICATOR -> REGIME` logic with\n`INDICATOR + CONTEXT + CROSS-MARKET CONFIRMATION -> REGIME INTERPRETATION`.\nThe same market move can mean different things depending on liquidity, credit,\nfunding, volatility and curve context. Distinguish PRICE MOVEMENT from SYSTEMIC\nTRANSMISSION. Where the data doesn't clearly confirm either a benign or a stress\nread, say so explicitly (AMBIGUOUS / MIXED / NEUTRAL) rather than forcing a label.\n\n## 1. Long-end yield breakout\n\n5.5% on the 30Y is a WATCH LEVEL, not a standalone regime trigger.\n`LONG-END STRESS CONFIRMED` requires 30Y > 5.5% AND at least one of:\nadverse 2s30s/2s10s steepening, MOVE rising materially, weak long-duration\nauctions, dealer take-down rising, credit spreads widening, repo/funding stress.\nA 30Y breach with falling short rates, tight credit, contained MOVE and healthy\nfunding is NOT risk-off on its own.\n\n## 2. Two types of bear steepening\n\nBENIGN/ROTATIONARY: long-end yields rise, credit stays tight, MOVE contained,\nfunding healthy, auctions clear normally, cyclicals/value/banks or broad\nequities strengthen. Reads as growth/reflation/rotation, not systemic stress.\n\nSTRESS/FISCAL: long-end yields rise sharply, MOVE rises, credit widens,\nauctions weaken, dealer take-down increases, funding stress appears. Reads as\nduration-absorption / fiscal / liquidity stress — high risk-off significance.\n\nIf stress signals are absent but the rotation-confirming signal (equity\nstrength) hasn't shown up either, classify as AMBIGUOUS rather than forcing\neither label.\n\n## 3. Bitcoin / crypto\n\nEvaluate real yields + USD + net/global liquidity + financial conditions +\ncredit/funding together, never real yields alone.\n\n- BTC HEADWIND: real yields UP AND USD UP AND liquidity flat/down AND/OR\n  financial conditions tighten.\n- BTC MIXED: real yields UP BUT liquidity expands AND USD stable/down AND\n  credit/funding healthy. Real yields may rise modestly without invalidating\n  a bullish liquidity signal.\n- BTC TAILWIND: liquidity UP strongly, USD DOWN, financial conditions ease,\n  credit stable.\n- If none of the above cleanly fit (e.g. everything is roughly flat), use\n  BTC MIXED/NEUTRAL — no strong directional signal.\n\n## 4. Gold\n\n- GOLD HEADWIND: real yields UP AND USD UP AND breakevens stable/down.\n- GOLD MIXED/POSITIVE: real yields UP BUT breakevens rise strongly, OR USD\n  falls materially, OR fiscal/currency credibility stress rises.\n- If drivers are roughly flat, use GOLD NEUTRAL rather than defaulting to a\n  headwind read.\n\n## 5. Speculative / long-duration growth (QQQ module)\n\nDo not label speculative long-duration equities a headwind solely because\nreal yields rise.\n\n- VALUATION HEADWIND: real yields UP (alone).\n- SYSTEMIC HEADWIND: real yields UP + credit spreads UP + funding tightens +\n  liquidity deteriorates, together.\n\nStrong liquidity and tight credit can offset valuation pressure even when\nreal yields are elevated.\n\n## 6. Weak-balance-sheet companies\n\nDepends primarily on credit spreads, refinancing conditions, lending\nstandards, funding costs and default expectations — not on the prevailing\ntightening-regime label. Do not mark this a generic headwind under every\nControlled Tightening regime; only when the credit/funding inputs above are\nactually deteriorating. If lending-standards/default-expectations data isn't\nbeing tracked, say so explicitly and treat it as a watch item, not a trigger.\n\n## 7. Risk-off transition — grouped confirmation\n\n`docs/data/latest.json -> v2.distance_to_transition.risk_off` is grouped, not\na flat equal-weighted checklist:\n\n- GROUP A — Duration / Treasury stress (bear steepening confirmed as\n  STRESS/FISCAL, Long-End Stress Confirmed, weak auctions, dealer take-down up)\n- GROUP B — Market-functioning stress (MOVE spike, repo/funding stress,\n  reserves deterioration)\n- GROUP C — Credit confirmation (HY/IG spreads widen materially)\n\nA confirmed LIQUIDITY CONTRACTION / RISK-OFF transition requires at least one\nmeaningful signal from Group A PLUS at least one from Group B or Group C.\nNever flip the regime off a single threshold or a single group.\n\n## Data schema notes\n\n- `v2.distance_to_transition.risk_off.groups.{a,b,c}` each carry\n  `{label, met, total, conditions:[{label, active}]}`.\n- `v2.distance_to_transition.risk_off.overall_confirmed` is true only when the\n  Group A + (B or C) rule is satisfied.\n- `v2.curve_topology.steepening_type` / `.steepening_basis` / `.steepening_read`\n  are populated whenever `classification` is a steepening variant; they carry\n  the BENIGN/ROTATIONARY vs STRESS/FISCAL vs AMBIGUOUS read.\n- `v2.gold_module.classification`, `v2.crypto_module.classification` carry the\n  headwind/mixed/tailwind (or neutral) verdict; `current_read` explains it\n  against all required factors, not just real yields.\n- `v2.qqq_module.distinction` / `.current_read` carry the valuation-vs-systemic\n  read for speculative long-duration equities.\n- `v2.trade_posture_v2.headwinds` entries must state their triggering\n  conditions and whether those conditions are currently met — never a bare\n  regime-based label.\n\n## Rendering\n\n`docs/index.html` is a pure template — it renders whatever these fields say.\nThe logic above must be applied when the fields are written (each refresh),\nthe same way every time. `index.html`'s only code-level change from this\ncorrection is the Risk-Off Transition panel (now renders 3 groups + an\noverall-confirmed badge) and a conditional STEEPENING TYPE line under the\nCurve Topology panel.\n## Mechanism data sources (added 2026-08-31)\n\nThree previously-unwired "Mechanism Data Quality" inputs now have confirmed live sources.\nWire these into `v2.data_freshness`, `v2.data_freshness_mechanism.rows` (recompute\n`stale_fraction`/`quality`), `v2.shock_absorbers_table`, and `v2.structural_demand`\neach refresh, the same way FRED/TreasuryDirect series are already pulled.\n\n### SOMA bill/Treasury composition\nSource: https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/soma-holdings (also has a JSON/CSV export\nAPI under markets.newyorkfed.org/api/soma/). Weekly, Wednesday level, published Thursday.\nGives Fed's own SOMA holdings by security type (T-Bills, Notes/Bonds, FRNs, TIPS,\nAgency, MBS, CMBS) and total. This is the Fed's own portfolio mix, not Treasury's\nissuance mix - keep the two concepts separate.\n\n### Treasury buybacks / issuance composition\nSource: https://www.treasurydirect.gov/auctions/announcements-data-results/buy-backs/\n(the old /instit/annceresult/... and /auctions/buybacks/ / /auctions/buy-backs URLs\nare dead after TreasuryDirect's site redesign - use this path). Table of buyback\noperations (date, type - Liquidity Support vs Cash Management -, security type,\nmaturity bucket), exportable to CSV. Confirms whether the buyback mechanism is\nactively running (it has been, roughly weekly through 2026). Actual Treasury\nissuance mix (bills vs coupons vs CMB) is a separate, still-unwired input that\nrequires the quarterly refunding financing tables.\n\n### FIMA Repo Facility usage\nSource: FRED series H41RESPPALGTRFNWW ("Assets: Other: Repurchase Agreements -\nForeign Official: Wednesday Level", H.4.1, weekly). This IS the FIMA Repo Facility\nbalance per the Fed's April 2020 H.4.1 methodology change (FIMA repos are reported\nunder Repurchase Agreements -> Foreign Official; ordinary dealer repos are reported\nunder Others). Optional: H41RESPPALGTRFXAWNWW (week-average variant) for context.\n\nDo NOT confuse with WLRRAFOIAL (Reverse Repurchase Agreements: Foreign Official and\nInternational Accounts) - that is the foreign-official RRP/investment pool, a\nLIABILITY where foreign officials place dollars WITH the Fed. FIMA repo is the\nopposite direction: an ASSET where the Fed lends dollars TO foreign officials\nagainst Treasury collateral.\n\nFIMA signal logic - usage is NOT a conventional "higher = better" shock absorber:\n- Facility existence/availability is structurally supportive on its own.\n- Usage = $0 -> DORMANT: backstop available, no evidence of foreign-official dollar\n  stress. This is the normal, healthy state, not a warning.\n- Usage > $0 -> ACTIVATED: at least one FIMA counterparty tapped the backstop.\n  Flag for review, do not read as bullish liquidity support.\n- Material/sustained usage -> GLOBAL DOLLAR / TREASURY LIQUIDITY WATCH.\n- Rapidly rising usage -> STRESS ESCALATION.\nReport backstop status and usage level as two distinct things, not one blended score.\n\nOptional context signal: WMTSECL1 ("Custody Holdings: Marketable U.S. Treasury\nSecurities", weekly) tracks foreign-official Treasury custody separately. Do not\nmathematically combine it with FIMA usage; use it qualitatively -\ncustody falling + FIMA usage still $0 suggests outright selling rather than\nfinancing through the facility; custody falling + FIMA usage rising suggests\nofficial holders are financing rather than selling outright.
