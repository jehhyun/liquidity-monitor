# Liquidity Monitor — Conditional Logic Methodology\n\n_Last corrected: 2026-08-31. This file is the durable reference for how risk-off\ntriggers and asset headwinds are classified. Any future refresh (manual or\nscheduled) should follow this, not single-indicator thresholds._\n\n## Core principle\n\nReplace binary `INDICATOR -> REGIME` logic with\n`INDICATOR + CONTEXT + CROSS-MARKET CONFIRMATION -> REGIME INTERPRETATION`.\nThe same market move can mean different things depending on liquidity, credit,\nfunding, volatility and curve context. Distinguish PRICE MOVEMENT from SYSTEMIC\nTRANSMISSION. Where the data doesn't clearly confirm either a benign or a stress\nread, say so explicitly (AMBIGUOUS / MIXED / NEUTRAL) rather than forcing a label.\n\n## 1. Long-end yield breakout\n\n5.5% on the 30Y is a WATCH LEVEL, not a standalone regime trigger.\n`LONG-END STRESS CONFIRMED` requires 30Y > 5.5% AND at least one of:\nadverse 2s30s/2s10s steepening, MOVE rising materially, weak long-duration\nauctions, dealer take-down rising, credit spreads widening, repo/funding stress.\nA 30Y breach with falling short rates, tight credit, contained MOVE and healthy\nfunding is NOT risk-off on its own.\n\n## 2. Two types of bear steepening\n\nBENIGN/ROTATIONARY: long-end yields rise, credit stays tight, MOVE contained,\nfunding healthy, auctions clear normally, cyclicals/value/banks or broad\nequities strengthen. Reads as growth/reflation/rotation, not systemic stress.\n\nSTRESS/FISCAL: long-end yields rise sharply, MOVE rises, credit widens,\nauctions weaken, dealer take-down increases, funding stress appears. Reads as\nduration-absorption / fiscal / liquidity stress — high risk-off significance.\n\nIf stress signals are absent but the rotation-confirming signal (equity\nstrength) hasn't shown up either, classify as AMBIGUOUS rather than forcing\neither label.\n\n## 3. Bitcoin / crypto\n\nEvaluate real yields + USD + net/global liquidity + financial conditions +\ncredit/funding together, never real yields alone.\n\n- BTC HEADWIND: real yields UP AND USD UP AND liquidity flat/down AND/OR\n  financial conditions tighten.\n- BTC MIXED: real yields UP BUT liquidity expands AND USD stable/down AND\n  credit/funding healthy. Real yields may rise modestly without invalidating\n  a bullish liquidity signal.\n- BTC TAILWIND: liquidity UP strongly, USD DOWN, financial conditions ease,\n  credit stable.\n- If none of the above cleanly fit (e.g. everything is roughly flat), use\n  BTC MIXED/NEUTRAL — no strong directional signal.\n\n## 4. Gold\n\n- GOLD HEADWIND: real yields UP AND USD UP AND breakevens stable/down.\n- GOLD MIXED/POSITIVE: real yields UP BUT breakevens rise strongly, OR USD\n  falls materially, OR fiscal/currency credibility stress rises.\n- If drivers are roughly flat, use GOLD NEUTRAL rather than defaulting to a\n  headwind read.\n\n## 5. Speculative / long-duration growth (QQQ module)\n\nDo not label speculative long-duration equities a headwind solely because\nreal yields rise.\n\n- VALUATION HEADWIND: real yields UP (alone).\n- SYSTEMIC HEADWIND: real yields UP + credit spreads UP + funding tightens +\n  liquidity deteriorates, together.\n\nStrong liquidity and tight credit can offset valuation pressure even when\nreal yields are elevated.\n\n## 6. Weak-balance-sheet companies\n\nDepends primarily on credit spreads, refinancing conditions, lending\nstandards, funding costs and default expectations — not on the prevailing\ntightening-regime label. Do not mark this a generic headwind under every\nControlled Tightening regime; only when the credit/funding inputs above are\nactually deteriorating. If lending-standards/default-expectations data isn't\nbeing tracked, say so explicitly and treat it as a watch item, not a trigger.\n\n## 7. Risk-off transition — grouped confirmation\n\n`docs/data/latest.json -> v2.distance_to_transition.risk_off` is grouped, not\na flat equal-weighted checklist:\n\n- GROUP A — Duration / Treasury stress (bear steepening confirmed as\n  STRESS/FISCAL, Long-End Stress Confirmed, weak auctions, dealer take-down up)\n- GROUP B — Market-functioning stress (MOVE spike, repo/funding stress,\n  reserves deterioration)\n- GROUP C — Credit confirmation (HY/IG spreads widen materially)\n\nA confirmed LIQUIDITY CONTRACTION / RISK-OFF transition requires at least one\nmeaningful signal from Group A PLUS at least one from Group B or Group C.\nNever flip the regime off a single threshold or a single group.\n\n## Data schema notes\n\n- `v2.distance_to_transition.risk_off.groups.{a,b,c}` each carry\n  `{label, met, total, conditions:[{label, active}]}`.\n- `v2.distance_to_transition.risk_off.overall_confirmed` is true only when the\n  Group A + (B or C) rule is satisfied.\n- `v2.curve_topology.steepening_type` / `.steepening_basis` / `.steepening_read`\n  are populated whenever `classification` is a steepening variant; they carry\n  the BENIGN/ROTATIONARY vs STRESS/FISCAL vs AMBIGUOUS read.\n- `v2.gold_module.classification`, `v2.crypto_module.classification` carry the\n  headwind/mixed/tailwind (or neutral) verdict; `current_read` explains it\n  against all required factors, not just real yields.\n- `v2.qqq_module.distinction` / `.current_read` carry the valuation-vs-systemic\n  read for speculative long-duration equities.\n- `v2.trade_posture_v2.headwinds` entries must state their triggering\n  conditions and whether those conditions are currently met — never a bare\n  regime-based label.\n\n## Rendering\n\n`docs/index.html` is a pure template — it renders whatever these fields say.\nThe logic above must be applied when the fields are written (each refresh),\nthe same way every time. `index.html`'s only code-level change from this\ncorrection is the Risk-Off Transition panel (now renders 3 groups + an\noverall-confirmed badge) and a conditional STEEPENING TYPE line under the\nCurve Topology panel.
